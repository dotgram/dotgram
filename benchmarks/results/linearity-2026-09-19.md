# Linearity, 2026-09-19 (rough)

`linearity` in the stand exe: every parser that reads a long input, timed at three sizes ten times
apart (warm 100 ms, then at least 200 ms and 3 calls per cell), one process, cores 16-31 on a busy
machine — the shape of the curve, not a number. The exponent of a step is `log(t2/t1) / log(n2/n1)`;
above 1.2 the series is flagged. Where the hand parser of the same input rises with it in the same
step (the stock count's good input, 1,000 to 10,000 lines) it is the allocator and the collector at
that size, not the parser's algorithm; where it does not, it is the parser.
