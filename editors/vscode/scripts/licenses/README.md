The licence texts `notices.mjs` writes for a package that names its licence by an SPDX expression and carries
no text of it: from SPDX's licence list, `text/<id>.txt` at tag v3.25.0
(https://github.com/spdx/license-list-data/tree/v3.25.0/text). A package under another licence and without a
text of its own fails the build until its text is added here.
